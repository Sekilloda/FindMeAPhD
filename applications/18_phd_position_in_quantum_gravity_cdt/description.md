## Original Vacancy Description

```text
Applications are invited for a three-years PhD position in the CDT formulation of quantum gravity using numerical simulations at the University of Graz/Austria.
The position is located in the group of Prof. Axel Maas, and funded by the FWF within the scope of the project 
"Matter-gravity interactions in CDT"
. The project aims at an understanding of the interaction of matter and gravity in CDT simulations. To this end, it will require to implement coupled, unquenched simulations and determine correlation functions, akin to the 
investigation of the pure gravity case
. This project is embedded within further investigations of the pure gravity case in CDT in the group, as well as other activities, which can be found at the 
group webpage
.
The starting date is flexible from 1st of February 2027, but preferably as early as possible. The position is fixed-term for the period of 3 years, with a possible extension of up to half a year, depending on the budget situation. The position is 30 hours/week, with initial gross annual salary 39649.40. The position includes the full social benefits in Austria, e.g. health care insurance and unemployment insurance. The position is equipped with travel funds. The position is embedded in the 
Research Career Campus Consortium "Theoretical Particle Physics"
, which provides further benefits and training.
Other senior group leaders of the theoretical particle physics section at the University of Graz are currently Reinhard Alkofer, Gernot Eichmann, Leonid Glozman, Alessia Platania, and Dénes Sexty, and as a junior group leader from 01/27 Marc Schiffer. The section's central areas of expertise are strongly-interacting gauge field theories, hadron physics, QCD at finite temperature and density, Higgs physics, BSM physics, quantum gravity, and both continuum and lattice methods are employed. The section also maintains the aforementioned doctoral academy consortium "Theoretical Particle Physics" providing a structured PhD education program since 2006. Hence an active surrounding in all aspects of particle physics is present.
Applicants should hold a master degree (at the starting date) in physics and they should have already shown good research abilities and potential in the field of quantum field theory and/or quantum gravity. Prior knowledge of CDT/EDT and/or lattice gauge theory are advantageous, but not required. The applicants are expected to actively participate in the scientific life of the Theoretical Physics Department.
If you are interested, please submit your application documents, preferably before the 13th of November 2026, directly to 
axel.maas@uni-graz.at
. Also questions can be directed to this address. Interviews are scheduled to take place in the second half of November online. The required application documents are a CV, a letter of motivation including a statement of research experiences and interests, a list of publications and talks (only if available), relevant certificates, the master thesis or an outline of it, and contact details of at least two possible referees.
We strive to increase the proportion of women and other underrepresented groups in physics, and therefore particularly encourage qualified persons from these groups to apply. To reduce implicit bias, please do not provide information on your country of origin, age, gender identity or a photograph of you in your motivation letter or CV, or in any file names. You can also use an anonymized email address and suppress your given name and/or provide a pseudonym, also for communication with the referees. The documents requiring identifying information, certificates, thesis and publication/talk list, will only be considered after an initial screening of the other documents.
```
