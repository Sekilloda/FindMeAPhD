## Original Vacancy Description

```text
The Department of Dosimetry and Application of Ionizing Radiation, Faculty of Nuclear Sciences and Physical Engineering, Czech Technical University in Prague, offers a PhD position in experimental hadron physics within the COMPASS Collaboration at CERN.
The project continues the COMPASS measurement of the hard exclusive π0 muoproduction cross section from the 2016 data (Phys. Lett. B 870 (2025) 139832), which indicates a dominant role of the chiral-odd GPD E_T. The candidate will measure the cross section with the not yet analysed 2017 data (160 GeV/c polarised µ+ and µ− beams, liquid-hydrogen target with recoil-proton detector), extract the contributions σT + εσL, σTT and σLT as functions of |t|, Q² and ν, combine the result with the 2016 data and compare it with GPD models. A possible extension is the extraction of SDMEs of vector mesons from the same data.
Requirements: Master’s degree in physics or a related field (ideally nuclear or particle physics) by the start of studies; programming in C++ and/or Python, ROOT experience is an advantage; good English.
We offer: membership in COMPASS, stays at CERN, conferences, and a total monthly income of at least CZK 30,000 (approx. EUR 1,200), likely higher, combining a doctoral stipend and a 0.5 FTE employment contract. Funding: FORTE project (CZ.02.01.01/00/22_008/0004632), co-funded by the EU. Doctoral programme: Nuclear Engineering, 4 years, start 1 March 2027.
More information about the application: kamil.augsten@fjfi.cvut.cz.
https://fjfi.cvut.cz/en/analysis-exclusive-meson-muoproduction-compass-experiment
```
