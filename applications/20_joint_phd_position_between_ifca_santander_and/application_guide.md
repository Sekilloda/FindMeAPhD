## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

When invited to an interview or virtual meeting with Dr. Bernal and Prof. Angulo, ask questions that demonstrate technical depth, methodological awareness, and genuine engagement with the project’s computational trajectory.

1. **Emulator Architecture & Physical Priors**  
   *“Regarding the development of emulators for diffuse emission and cross-correlation pipelines, are you currently favoring Gaussian process regression, normalizing flows, or physics-informed neural networks? How do you plan to embed physical constraints (e.g., conservation laws, bias models, redshift-space distortions) to ensure stable extrapolation beyond training domains?”*

2. **Simulation-Based Inference & Likelihood Evaluation**  
   *“For likelihood-free inference on line-intensity maps, how do you currently handle the computational bottleneck of repeated forward modeling? Would you be open to exploring active learning, adaptive sampling, or quantum-inspired variational methods to accelerate the emulator training loop for SKA/SphereX-scale datasets?”*

3. **HPC Workflow & Cross-Institutional Integration**  
   *“Given the joint IFCA–DIPC structure and access to large-scale HPC facilities, what is the current workflow for distributing simulation tasks and managing data versioning across clusters? Are there plans to implement containerized reproducibility pipelines (e.g., Docker/Singularity) or workflow managers (e.g., Nextflow, Snakemake) to streamline emulator generation?”*

4. **Theoretical Interface: Discrete vs. Diffuse Components**  
   *“In modeling the interface between discrete galaxy populations and diffuse emission, how do you currently treat shot noise, clustering systematics, and non-linear bias in the emulator’s latent space? Would you consider hybrid approaches that combine semi-analytic galaxy formation models with machine-learning surrogates for the diffuse component?”*
