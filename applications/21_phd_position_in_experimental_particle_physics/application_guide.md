## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Asking technically sharp, project-specific questions demonstrates genuine engagement and positions you as a collaborative researcher. Use these during the interview or in follow-up correspondence:

1. **Trigger Algorithm & Latency Optimization:**  
   *"Regarding the development of new trigger algorithms for real-time analysis, how does the collaboration currently balance the strict latency constraints of the DAQ system with the need for high-fidelity reconstruction of missing energy/momentum signatures? Are there plans to integrate lightweight ML models or surrogate algorithms to accelerate trigger decisions without compromising sensitivity?"*

2. **Monte Carlo Simulation Bottlenecks:**  
   *"Dark sector searches at NA64 often involve low-mass, weakly coupled signatures that are computationally expensive to simulate in Geant4. How does the group currently address simulation bottlenecks, and would there be scope to explore quantum-inspired acceleration, variance reduction techniques, or neural surrogate modeling for detector response?"*

3. **Detector Upgrades & Sensitivity Roadmap:**  
   *"With the upcoming CERN shutdown and the optimization phase for NA64's next run, what specific upgrades to the electromagnetic calorimeter, tracking systems, or beam instrumentation are prioritized to enhance sensitivity to light dark matter? How will these hardware changes feed into the reconstruction software and trigger logic?"*

4. **Background Discrimination & Statistical Frameworks:**  
   *"In the electron and muon channels, irreducible SM backgrounds can mimic dark sector signals. How does the collaboration currently approach background estimation and signal extraction, and are there opportunities to implement advanced statistical methods (e.g., likelihood-free inference, Bayesian neural networks, or anomaly detection frameworks) to improve discrimination power?"*
