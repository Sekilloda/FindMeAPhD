Emphasize your experience simulating complex particle cascades and how those computational frameworks transfer to neutrino detector response modeling.

Position yourself as a candidate who can immediately contribute to the simulation pipeline while bringing fresh algorithmic perspectives to data analysis.

**Strategic Narrative Arc:**

## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Prepare these for a technical interview with Dr. Carmen Palomares or the CIEMAT Neutrino Group. They demonstrate deep engagement with LiquidO physics, simulation challenges, and modern experimental neutrino trends.

1. **Light Propagation & Simulation Fidelity:**  
   *"Opaque scintillators like LiquidO present unique challenges in modeling stochastic light propagation and scintillation yield. How does the group currently parameterize photon transport and re-emission in the simulation framework, and are there plans to integrate quantum-inspired or ML-driven surrogate models to accelerate detector response simulations without sacrificing fidelity?"*

2. **Energy Resolution & Background Rejection:**  
   *"For reactor neutrino applications, precise low-energy reconstruction and robust background rejection are critical. What are the group’s primary strategies for optimizing the LiquidO detector’s energy resolution in the 1–10 MeV regime, particularly in distinguishing inverse beta decay signals from environmental gamma backgrounds?"*

3. **Validation & Benchmarking:**  
   *"Given the group’s involvement in ProtoDUNE and DUNE/SBND, how does the LiquidO simulation pipeline validate detector response against established benchmarks? Are there opportunities to cross-calibrate LiquidO response models using existing CERN Neutrino Platform or Fermilab test beam data?"*

4. **Data Analysis & Algorithmic Innovation:**  
   *"Modern neutrino experiments increasingly rely on advanced statistical methods and pattern recognition for event reconstruction. Would there be scope to contribute to the data analysis pipeline using novel algorithmic approaches—such as quantum-inspired optimization or graph-based neural networks—for track reconstruction and background suppression in opaque scintillator data?"*
