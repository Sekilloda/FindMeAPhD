Emphasize that studying hadronization histories exposed you to the limitations of perturbative expansions and the necessity of non-perturbative mathematical structures (instantons, transseries, large-N expansions).
2. **Quantum Computing as a Methodological Asset:** Frame QC not as a replacement for analytical work, but as a complementary probe for non-perturbative QFT. Highlight how quantum algorithms, witness circuits, and tensor-network-inspired sampling can validate transseries resummations, explore spectral properties of random matrix ensembles, or simulate gauge theory path integrals on near-term hardware.
3. **Mathematical Physics Fluency:** Stress your comfort with asymptotic analysis, special functions, differential equations, and numerical methods. Prof. Schiappa’s group values mathematical rigor; demonstrate that your computational work is grounded in analytical precision.
4. **Logistical & Financial Alignment:** Portugal is EU/EEA; your dual citizenship grants domestic status, eliminating visa delays and qualifying you for both FCT and *la Caixa* pathways. Standard EU doctoral funding in Portugal typically meets or exceeds your >€2,000 net/month threshold (FCT allowances + tax exemptions; *la Caixa* stipends ~€2,500–3,000 gross/month).
