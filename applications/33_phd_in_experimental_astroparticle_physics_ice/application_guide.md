## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Asking technically precise questions demonstrates preparation, physics maturity, and genuine interest in the project's trajectory. Ask 3–4 during the interview.

1. **Hadronic Model Constraints:**  
   *"The extended IceTop array will provide coincident radio and scintillation data to test hadronic interaction models. How do you plan to disentangle model-dependent systematic uncertainties (e.g., between EPOS-LHC, QGSJET-II, and Sibyll 2.3) when fitting the longitudinal shower profiles, and what role will the new surface detector calibration play in reducing these degeneracies?"*  
   *(Why it works: Shows you understand the core physics challenge and the interplay between detector calibration and phenomenological modeling.)*

2. **Reconstruction & Algorithmic Integration:**  
   *"Given the hybrid nature of the upgraded IceTop system, what are the primary bottlenecks in synchronizing and reconstructing signals across radio antennas, scintillators, and the in-ice optical modules? Are there plans to integrate machine learning or advanced statistical reconstruction techniques to improve event-by-event energy and direction resolution?"*  
   *(Why it works: Bridges your computational background with experimental needs, showing you're thinking about modern data-analysis pipelines.)*

3. **Theoretical-Experimental Synergy:**  
   *"KIT's KCETA environment features strong theoretical phenomenology groups. How does the KSETA program facilitate cross-pollination between experimental astroparticle researchers and theorists working on QCD modeling and cosmic-ray simulations? Are there structured opportunities for PhD students to contribute to joint phenomenological studies?"*  
   *(Why it works: Demonstrates awareness of the institutional ecosystem and aligns with your interest in theory-experiment interfaces.)*

4. **Future Scientific Roadmap:**  
   *"Looking beyond hadronic interaction tests, the upgraded surface detector is expected to lay the groundwork for future gamma-ray searches. What are the key experimental milestones or analysis frameworks you envision developing over the next 3–5 years to enable high-precision multi-messenger gamma-ray astronomy with IceTop?"*  
   *(Why it works: Shows forward-thinking, strategic interest in the collaboration's long-term goals, and positions you as a candidate invested in the project's evolution.)*
