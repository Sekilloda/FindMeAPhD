My coursework and research have deepened my command of effective field theory, statistical inference, and tensor-network methods, all of which I am eager to apply to dark matter phenomenology and astroparticle constraints.

**Administrative Ease & Commitment**

I am seeking a fully funded PhD position with a monthly compensation aligned with standard EU doctoral stipends, and I am prepared to contribute from day one to both analytical and computational aspects of your projects. Are there ongoing internal workshops or shared computational resources that new PhD students typically engage with during their first year?"*
