## Original Vacancy Description

```text
Expressions of interest are invited from prospective PhD students interested in pursuing research in 
dark matter phenomenology and astroparticle physics
 in Torino, Italy, through the joint programme between the 
University of Turin (UniTO)
 and the 
China Scholarship Council (CSC)
.
The opportunity is intended for candidates eligible for the CSC programme who wish to apply for admission to the 
PhD Programme in Physics at the University of Turin
 and carry out their research in close collaboration with 
Dr. Mattia Di Mauro (INFN Torino)
 and other members of the Astroparticle Physics group.
The proposed PhD research will focus on theoretical and phenomenological aspects of particle dark matter, with particular emphasis on the phenomenology of 
non-standard WIMP scenarios
. The precise research project will be defined together with the selected candidate, taking into account their interests and background.
The student will work primarily with 
Dr. Mattia Di Mauro at INFN Torino
, while being fully integrated into the broader particle and astroparticle physics environment of the 
Department of Physics of the University of Turin and INFN Torino
. The position will therefore offer opportunities for interaction and collaboration with UniTO faculty members, INFN researchers, PhD students, and international collaborators working on dark matter, particle physics, cosmic rays, gamma rays, neutrinos, and astroparticle physics.
This advertisement is an 
expression of interest
 associated with the University of Turin--China Scholarship Council programme. It is not a separate recruitment call or an independently funded PhD position. The China Scholarship Council provides scholarships for eligible Chinese students wishing to pursue a PhD abroad, while the University of Turin carries out an annual pre-selection of candidates applying to its PhD programmes through the CSC scheme.
More information about the programme is available at:
https://en.unito.it/research/opportunities-research-careers/phd/internationalization-phd-courses/china-scholarship
Interested candidates are encouraged to first contact 
Dr. Mattia Di Mauro
 to discuss their scientific interests and the possibility of developing a PhD research project for the next UniTO--CSC selection.
```
