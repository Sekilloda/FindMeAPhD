## Original Vacancy Description

```text
The Experimental Particle Physics and Gravity (EPPG) group at Ghent University (UGent) has an opening for a joint PhD candidate position between UGent and İstanbul Nişantaşı Üniversitesi, Istanbul, Turkey, to work on the SHiP (Search for Hidden Particles) experiment at the Beam Dump Facility at CERN.
The EPPG group is well-known for its prominent role in the CMS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), particularly for its strong contributions to the physics analysis of the top quark and Higgs boson sectors. The group has led the first observations of several rare physics processes involving top quarks, establishing deep expertise in searching for physics beyond the Standard Model. Building on this extensive research program, the group is now playing a key role in the SHiP collaboration, which represents a major direction for the group at the high-intensity frontier. The members of the EPPG group are also actively involved in other major international projects, such as ET, LIGO/VIRGO, IceCube, RNO-G, Project8, and SoLid.
A successful candidate will join the EPPG group to contribute to the physics reach studies of the SHiP experiment. The work will involve the development of analysis techniques using simulated data to explore the experiment’s sensitivity to neutrino physics and various new physics scenarios beyond the Standard Model. In addition to these physics studies, the candidate will contribute to SHiP instrumentation through detector prototyping and test beam campaigns to evaluate and validate performance requirements.
The position is proposed for four years, starting as soon as possible. The application must include the CV of the candidate and two letters of reference. The candidate must hold a master’s degree in physics. The complete applications should be sent directly to Prof. Didar Dobur (didar.dobur@ugent.be) and Dr. Kirill Skovpen (kirill.skovpen@ugent.be) before the deadline of Nov 29, 2026. The submitted applications will be reviewed in the order of their reception.
```
