## Original Vacancy Description

```text
The group for rare event searches, jointly run by TU Wien and MBI, is a member of the direct dark matter detection experiments CRESST and COSINUS, the neutrino experiment NUCLEUS, and the low-energy calibration effort CRAB. The group is heavily involved in data analysis and simulation for these experiments and develops their DAQ systems.
We invite applications for a 4-year PhD position (30 h/week) to join the ACT project “Accurate Calibration for Cryo-detectors at TRIGA” at TU Wien. The successful candidate will work at the unique CRAB calibration facility, which couples a dilution cryostat with a pure thermal neutron beam and gamma-tagging detectors at the TRIGA Mark II reactor in Vienna. The candidate will play a key role in data analysis, software development, and Monte Carlo simulations for CRAB.
Keywords: Dark matter, Neutrino, (Astro-) particle physics, Data analysis, Monte Carlo simulations, cryogenic calorimeters, DAQ.
Tasks: 
- Data analysis and statistical inference
- R&D with a focus on CRAB
- Monte Carlo Simulations (with Geant4)
- Comparison of measurement with simulation
- On-site support at the atomic institute, TU Wien
- Participation in international conferences and summer schools (travel costs will be covered)
- Cooperation and day-to-day supervision of undergraduate students
- Optional: participation in hardware and software development of the data acquisition system
- Optional: contributions to the setup of the new cryolab
- Optional: tasks may include methods of machine learning
Required qualifications/skills:
- Scientific interest in particle physics, dark matter, and neutrino research
- Strong background in statistics and programming. Experience with scientific programming, preferably in Python or C++, is desirable; familiarity with other programming languages and tools is an advantage.
- Team-oriented and communication skills to work in an international and diverse collaboration
- Ability to perform independent, creative, and innovative research
- MSc or equivalent in physics, computer science, or related (completed or close to completion)
We encourage everyone who is interested in the position to apply! The TU Wien is an equal opportunity employer. We affirm diversity and work to create an inclusive working environment for everybody.
The position will be funded for four years. For this position, a minimum salary of € 2,832.07 gross per month (14x a year, pay grade B1 of the Austrian collective agreement for university staff) with an employment of 30 hours/week is offered, including full health and social insurance. Additional benefits for employees can be found in 
TU Wien's Fringe-Benefit Catalog
. More information about the Marietta Blau Institute for Particle Physics of the Austrian Academy of Sciences and the TU Wien may be found at: 
https://www.oeaw.ac.at/mbi
 and at:

, respectively.
Your application should include a CV, a research statement (max. 2 pages), and two people whom we may contact for further reference. Please send your application to Dr. Samir Banik (samir.banik@oeaw.ac.at) with the subject line "Application for PhD position – CRAB/ACT".
The deadline is 
November 30, 2026
```
