## Original Vacancy Description

```text
The Trapped Ion Quantum Technology Group at Stockholm University is recruiting a motivated doctoral candidate for experimental research on open quantum systems with trapped ions. The project studies open spin–boson systems and many-body phases, working toward applications in sensing and metrology, as part of the QuantERA project "Collective quantum phenomena in dissipative systems – towards time-crystal applications in sensing and metrology" (CoQuaDis, EU Horizon 2020 grant 101017733), in collaboration with the University of Tübingen and IFISC Mallorca.
Candidates should hold a Master's degree in physics (or be near completion), including at least 60 credits of second-cycle coursework and an independent degree project of at least 15 credits, with English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper-secondary level B.

https://qtech.fysik.su.se
```
