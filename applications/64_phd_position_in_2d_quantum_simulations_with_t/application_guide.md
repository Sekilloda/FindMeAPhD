## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Prepare these questions to demonstrate deep engagement with the group’s research, your theoretical-computational value, and the project’s trajectory. Ask 2–3 during the interview.

1. **Open-System Scaling & Control Optimization**  
   > *“As the 2D Rydberg ion array scales beyond 10–20 sites, crosstalk and non-Markovian decoherence will become dominant bottlenecks. Is the group currently integrating optimal control theory (e.g., GRAPE, CRAB) or machine learning–based pulse shaping to mitigate these effects, and would a doctoral candidate have the opportunity to develop theoretical control protocols alongside experimental implementation?”*

2. **Theoretical-Experimental Feedback Loop**  
   > *“Given the ERC Synergy collaboration with Mainz and Tübingen, how is the modeling of open-system dynamics integrated into the experimental control loop? Are there plans to develop analytical or tensor-network models for the observed dissipation pathways, and could my background in quantum algorithms for hadronization histories contribute to designing effective Hamiltonians or measurement observables for the simulator?”*

3. **Connection to Gauge Theories & Non-Equilibrium Dynamics**  
   > *“The Lund string model and hadronization involve string breaking, particle production, and non-equilibrium thermalization—processes that share structural similarities with driven-dissipative many-body systems. Are there near-term plans to map these open-system dynamics to analog quantum simulations, and how might a candidate with a QCD phenomenology background help bridge the gap between particle physics-inspired models and trapped-ion control architectures?”*

4. **Project Trajectory & Candidate Role**  
   > *“How does the OPEN-2QS project envision transitioning from proof-of-concept 2D simulations to scalable, fault-tolerant quantum simulation architectures? In what ways do doctoral candidates typically shape the theoretical or algorithmic direction of the project, and what milestones would you expect a successful candidate to deliver within the first 18 months?”*
