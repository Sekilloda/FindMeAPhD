## Original Vacancy Description

```text
The Trapped Ion Quantum Technology Group at Stockholm University is recruiting a motivated doctoral candidate for research in quantum simulations with trapped Rydberg ions. The project involves building and operating a two-dimensional ion trap experiment for open-system quantum simulations using Rydberg ions, as part of the ERC Synergy project "Open 2D Quantum Simulator" (OPEN-2QS, grant 101164443), in collaboration with groups in Mainz and Tübingen.
Candidates should hold a Master's degree in physics (or be near completion), including at least 60 credits of second-cycle coursework and an independent degree project of at least 15 credits, with English proficiency equivalent to Swedish upper-secondary level B.
Further details on the group are available at
https://qtech.fysik.su.se
.
```
