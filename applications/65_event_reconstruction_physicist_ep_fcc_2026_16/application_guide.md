- **Position yourself as a "Physics-Informed Algorithm Developer"**: Emphasize that your theoretical training gives you an intuitive grasp of *what* is reconstructible, while your computational/QC work demonstrates *how* to model it efficiently.

However, CERN maintains formal PhD pathways through partner universities (e.g., University of Geneva, EPFL, UCLouvain). ## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Prepare these to demonstrate deep engagement with the vacancy’s technical scope and CERN’s strategic direction. Ask 3–4 during the interview, tailoring to the supervisor’s responses.

1. **Graph Scalability & FCC Geometry**  
   > *"The vacancy mentions extending the data model with multi-surface boundary crossings and sparse association storage to handle pileup consistently. In your experience, what are the primary bottlenecks when scaling this graph structure to FCC-ee’s higher luminosity and denser event topologies? Are you leaning toward hierarchical graph partitioning, dynamic memory pooling, or GPU-accelerated sparse indexing to maintain navigation latency?"*

2. **ML Integration & Truth Association**  
   > *"You’re applying graph neural networks and transformers to clustering and particle identification. How do you currently validate that ML-driven associations preserve physical conservation laws (e.g., charge, energy-momentum, vertex consistency) compared to traditional rule-based associators? Are you planning to embed physical constraints directly into the GNN architecture, or rely on post-hoc truth-graph validation metrics?"*

3. **PhD Pathway & Supervision Structure**  
   > *"CERN’s research staff positions often align with external doctoral programs. Could you clarify the expected PhD trajectory for this role? Is there a preferred partner university, and how would the supervision split between the EP-FCC group and the academic supervisor? I’m particularly interested in how the truth-graph generalization work could be structured into a publishable, thesis-worthy contribution."*

4. **Benchmarking & Portability**  
   > *"The role emphasizes portability across CPUs and GPUs using frameworks like Alpaka or SYCL. When benchmarking the new reconstruction algorithms against the current CMS/HGCAL pipelines, what are the primary trade-offs you’re optimizing for: inference latency, memory footprint, or reconstruction accuracy? Are you planning to use the truth graph itself as a differentiable simulator for end-to-end training, or will the ML models remain decoupled from the simulation stack?"*
