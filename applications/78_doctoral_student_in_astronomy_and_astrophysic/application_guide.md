## 1. Strategic Match & Research Angle

### The Core Challenge & Opportunity
The vacancy emphasizes **theoretical astrophysics and galactic-scale planet formation**, while your background is anchored in **QCD phenomenology, the Lund string model, and quantum algorithms**. At first glance, this appears misaligned. However, the overlap is profound when framed through the lens of **stochastic many-body dynamics, phase-space evolution, and computational modeling**.

### How to Bridge the Gap
| Your Background | Astrophysical Parallel | Strategic Pitch |
|----------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| **Lund String Model & Hadronization** | Stochastic planetesimal formation & ejection dynamics | Frame hadronization as a non-perturbative branching process analogous to gravitational scattering, accretion, and ejection trajectories in planetary systems. Both require modeling complex, non-linear phase-space evolution under stochastic constraints. |
| **Quantum Algorithms for Hadronization Histories** | High-dimensional parameter sampling & trajectory optimization | Position quantum circuit design as expertise in constructing efficient sampling algorithms for complex probability distributions. This translates directly to quantum-inspired Monte Carlo, tensor-network simulations, or quantum machine learning for galactic-scale parameter inference. |
| **Rank Indistinguishability Witness Circuits** | Statistical inference & symmetry-aware modeling | Highlight experience with symmetry-preserving quantum observables and witness-based validation. Map this to statistical frameworks for distinguishing formation channels, ejection mechanisms, or population synthesis models. |
| **Theoretical Particle Physics & QFT** | Mathematical rigor & dynamical systems | Emphasize comfort with Lagrangian/Hamiltonian formalisms, perturbation/non-perturbation theory, and analytical modeling. These are foundational to theoretical astrophysics, especially when deriving equations of motion for planetesimal populations or galactic potential models. |

### Recommended Narrative Arc
1. **Acknowledge the shift:** Frame your transition not as a departure, but as an expansion of theoretical/computational physics into a new dynamical regime.
2. **Highlight transferable methodology:** Stress that stochastic branching, phase-space sampling, and algorithmic design are domain-agnostic tools.
3. **Position quantum computing as a forward-looking asset:** Not as a replacement for classical N-body or hydrodynamic codes, but as a complementary toolkit for high-dimensional sampling, symmetry-aware inference, and next-generation computational astrophysics.
4. **Align with Lund's strengths:** Lund has a historic reputation in theoretical physics and computational modeling. Emphasize your readiness to contribute to the Planet Formation Group's analytical and numerical pipeline.
