## 4. Targeted Interview Questions

Prepare these for the technical interview or supervisor meeting. They demonstrate deep engagement with the project’s scope and WACQT’s ecosystem.

1. **On Anharmonic Dynamics & Non-Gaussian States**  
   > *"In leveraging anharmonic potentials for quantum sensing, how do you envision balancing the generation of non-Gaussian states with the need to maintain coherence times? Are you prioritizing specific anharmonicities (e.g., Duffing-type vs. transmon-like) to maximize the quantum Fisher information under realistic drive constraints?"*

2. **On Open-System Modeling & Non-Markovian Effects**  
   > *"When modeling open quantum systems for sensing protocols, how do you approach the trade-off between computational tractability (e.g., Lindblad vs. HEOM or tensor-network master equations) and the need to capture non-Markovian memory effects that often dominate in mechanical resonator experiments?"*

3. **On Distributed Sensing & Resource Scaling**  
   > *"The project mentions distributed quantum sensing networks. How do you expect the scaling of entanglement witnesses and quantum Fisher information to change when moving from single anharmonic sensors to coupled networks, and what are the primary decoherence channels that could limit network-wide metrological advantage?"*

4. **On WACQT Synergy & Experimental Feedback**  
   > *"Given WACQT’s strong experimental-theoretical integration, how do you plan to incorporate real-world constraints from mechanical resonator or superconducting circuit groups into protocol development? Are there near-term milestones where theoretical bounds could be directly benchmarked against ongoing experimental runs?"*
